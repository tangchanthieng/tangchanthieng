`Featured projects:`

### 01. Aurora Bank Datawarehouse Project
   
**Business domain:** Finance

**Business Problem:** Currently, Aurora Bank's data exists as separate operational files, making it difficult to perform comprehensive analysis without significant manual effort.

**Tools:** Python, SQL Server, MS Excel, Power BI, Trello, Git

**Key Analysis:** Clients, Cards, Merchants, and Transactions.

**Outcome:** An end-to-end analytics platform providing a data warehouse, etl pipeline, queries, and dashboard development to answer business questions and optimise operational performance, risk management, and leverage marketing outcomes.

### 02. SAS Curiosity Cup 2024 Global Competition

**Business domain:** Energy/Environment

**Business Problem:** Environmental concerns and climate change mitigation are paramount for modern institutions. During the 2018–2021 period, La Trobe university faced serious energy disruption as transitions to online learning fundamentally altered baseline demand.

**Tools:** Python, MS Excel, Machine Learning, SAS, Trello, Git

**Key Analysis:** Energy consumption pre-covid, during covid, and post-covid. Energy consumption before and after implementing solar/energy-saving applications.

**Outcome:** A complete report with details of machine learning results, visualisations, and recommendations regarding Net Zero 2029 goal.

### 03. NLP Sentiment Project

**Business domain:** Healthcare

**Business Problem:** Healthcare-related conversations are continuously generated across social media platforms, but the information is stored as free-text rather than structured datasets suitable for reporting. As customer opinions increasingly appear on social media platforms, organizations require analytical capabilities to monitor sentiment, understand customer concerns, and respond proactively to changing public expectations.

**Tools:** Python, NLP, MS Excel, Trello, Git

**Key Analysis:** Sentiment, topic modelling and analysis.

**Outcome:** A full-text analysis of NLP and Sentiment analysis from text normalisation, customer sentiment pattern detection, topic modelling and visualisation.
